# Asset credits

The replacement PINE RIDGE and TIMBER CAMP maps use [Poly Haven's Pine Forest collection](https://polyhaven.com/collections/pine_forest), licensed [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license): Fir Tree 01 and Fir Sapling by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers, Fern 02 by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers, Dead Tree Trunk by Rob Tuytel, and Forest Ground 04 by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers. The downloaded models are converted with Blender 5.2 by `tools/convert_woodland.py`; the playable layouts, collisions, spawn points and objectives are custom to IRONFRONT.

Every third-party asset in IRONFRONT is **CC0 (public domain)**, except the MIT-licensed three.js water normal map. Credit is not legally required, but these creators deserve it.
